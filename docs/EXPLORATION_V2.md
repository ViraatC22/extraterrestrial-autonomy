A calibrated learner would cover the truth about 95% of the time. v2 lander timeouts
are rovers waiting at the lander to recharge, not requesting help: the livelock is gone,
but a slow recharge can still run out the step budget.

**Degenerate conditions.** Under the engine shown, these conditions produced missions identical to plain Mars OOD, so they measure nothing extra:

- v2: `mars_comm_delay`

Communication delay only costs time when the rover requests help. v2 removes the ratchet and the livelock, the rover almost never asks, and with independent random streams nothing else differs. A v2 study would need a condition where delay actually binds.
